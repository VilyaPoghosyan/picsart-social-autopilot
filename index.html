Social Autopilot. Now with your own accounts and the numbers.

The next version of the prototype. Each person connects their own Instagram and TikTok, chooses where and when every approved reel goes, and gets a weekly report that the agents learn from.

1:55, recorded on 4 Nov 2026, with narration and sound. The connected Instagram account is real. After TikTok's sign-in page, the film uses practice accounts and simulated posts, marked "Simulated" on screen. The Reelo report shows real numbers.

What is new

  • Connect your own accounts realA Connect button on the Instagram and TikTok cards opens the platform's own sign-in. The password goes only to Instagram or TikTok; the app never sees it. Each person's accounts stay separate.
  • Approve with where, when and TikTok settings realEvery finished reel waits for approval. The owner picks the platforms, the time and who can see it on TikTok. The AI label is always on.
  • A posting queue realApproved reels wait in a queue, and each card follows its reel to live, with a link to every post.
  • A weekly report simulated in the filmViews, followers, the best and the weakest reel, and the cost per post. The agents suggest changes; the owner accepts or rejects each one, and the next week's plan follows.
  • One page for every channel realPosts made, posted, views and credits, side by side.

Not switched on yet

  • Automatic posting to people's own accounts. Approving puts a reel in the queue; nothing goes out by itself.
  • Stats from people's own accounts come once posting is on. The film shows simulated numbers, and real ones from Picsart's Reelo channel.
  • Like the first version, it runs on one Mac, so it opens only while it says online below.

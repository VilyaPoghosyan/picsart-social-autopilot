How it works

  1. Connect your accounts realA Connect button opens Instagram's or TikTok's own sign-in. The password goes only to the platform; the app never sees it.
  2. Describe the channel realA name, what it is about, who watches, the tone and a monthly budget. That is the whole brief.
  3. An agent writes the playbook realLook, palette, a recurring cast with portraits, story ideas and rules, in a few minutes. You can change any part before the first week.
  4. The agents make the week realSeven ideas with first frames, then finished reels. Every step shows its time and its credits.
  5. Every reel is checked realMeasured checks plus a critic that scores each reel. A reel that fails gets one retake, or it is dropped.
  6. You approve realWhere it goes, when, and who can see it on TikTok. The AI label is always on. Approved reels wait in a posting queue.
  7. A weekly report simulatedViews, followers, the best and the weakest reel, and the cost per post. You accept or reject each change the agents suggest.

Not switched on yet

  • Stats from people's own accounts come once posting is on. Until then the report uses simulated numbers, and the example report shows real ones from Picsart's Reelo channel.
  • A server. It runs on one Mac, so it is online only when Vilya starts it (see above).
